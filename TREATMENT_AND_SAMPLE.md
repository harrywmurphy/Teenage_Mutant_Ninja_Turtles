# Treatment Documentation & Sample Freeze (v1.1)
**Companion to PREREGISTRATION.md · September 17, 2026**

This file documents the treatment (the TMNT franchise as a repeated-dose exposure), the character canon used as measurement instruments, and the frozen unit roster. Amendments to the original pre-registration are marked **[AMENDED]** with reasons.

---

## 1. Dose schedule (franchise release history)

Treatment is not a single event but a repeated-dose exposure of varying intensity and medium. Doses, with approximate reach class:

| Year(s) | Dose | Medium | Reach class |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1984 | Mirage *TMNT* #1 (Eastman & Laird) | B&W indie comic | Niche (first print ~3k) |
| 1987–1996 | Original animated series (10 seasons, syndicated) | TV | **Mass — primary dose** |
| 1988– | Playmates toy line | Merchandise | Mass |
| 1990 / 1991 / 1993 | Live-action film trilogy | Film | Mass (1990 film ~$200M WW) |
| 1997–1998 | *Ninja Turtles: The Next Mutation* | TV | Minor |
| 2003–2009 | 4Kids animated series | TV | Mass (kids) |
| 2007 | *TMNT* (CGI film) | Film | Moderate |
| 2011– | IDW comics continuity | Comic | Niche |
| 2012–2017 | Nickelodeon CGI series | TV | Mass (kids) |
| 2014 / 2016 | Bay-produced films | Film | Mass (2014 film ~$490M WW) |
| 2018–2020 (+2022 film) | *Rise of the TMNT* | TV/streaming | Moderate |
| 2022 | *Shredder's Revenge* (game) | Video game | Moderate |
| 2023 | *Mutant Mayhem* (~$180M WW) + 2024 *Tales of the TMNT* | Film/TV | Mass — **the daily-resolution dose** (pageview era) |

Registered implication: the structural break should track the **1987–1990 mass doses**, not the 1984 origin. Re-dose/renewal analysis uses the film years; *Mutant Mayhem* is the only dose observable at daily resolution with clickstream context.

## 2. Character canon as measurement instruments

| Instrument | Canon status | Probe window | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Weapons** | **Most stable marker in the franchise.** In the 1984 comics all turtles wore red; *weapons were the only visual differentiator*. Katana→Leonardo, sai→Raphael, bō staff→Donatello, nunchaku→Michelangelo, held across essentially all major iterations | 1984– | **[AMENDED] Promoted to co-primary persona-bleed probe** (alongside the spelling fossil). Frozen queries: "[artist] katana/sai/bo staff/nunchucks"; Trends "[artist] weapon"; LLM battery: "What weapon do you associate with [artist], the Renaissance painter/sculptor?" |
| **Bandana colors** | Introduced by the **1987 cartoon/Playmates toys** (blue Leo, red Raph, purple Don, orange Mikey); not present in 1984 comics (all red). Early toy sketches briefly had Don-orange/Mikey-purple before standardizing (footnote). Kept in all subsequent major iterations | **1987–** | **[AMENDED] Color-probe treatment date corrected 1984 → 1987.** No color signal should predate the cartoon — this is a falsification check for the color probe itself |
| **Personalities** | Codified by the **1987 theme song** ("Leonardo leads, Donatello does machines, Raphael is cool but rude, Michelangelo is a party dude") — a citable canonical assignment text. Persona↔history fit: Raphael and Michelangelo personas *contradict* the historical men (identification units); Leonardo/Donatello loose fits | 1987– | Deviation era: *Rise of the TMNT* (2018–20) altered team dynamics and weapons before *Mutant Mayhem* reverted to classic canon — treated as minor measurement noise; details to verify during write-up |
| **Names/spelling** | Franchise used the misspelling **"Michaelangelo"** in its early era; later continuities standardized to the correct "Michelangelo" (correction timing to verify — likely 2000s) | 1984– | Refined prediction: misspelling share should rise post-1987 **and recede in recent decades** as the franchise corrected itself — a rise-and-fall fossil, not a permanent one |

## 3. Frozen unit roster **[AMENDED from pre-registration §3]**

Reason for amendment (dated, pre-formal-analysis): original control list included the treated artists' teachers (Verrocchio→Leonardo, Ghirlandaio→Michelangelo, Perugino→Raphael), whose book-mentions are mechanically chained to treated-artist coverage. Growth check 1970–83 → 1991–2005 confirmed linked controls run hot (1.36–1.62x) vs. clean controls (0.94–1.30x).

**Treated (4):** Leonardo da Vinci · Michelangelo · Raphael · Donatello. Primary case: Donatello.

**Primary clean controls (6):** Titian · Tintoretto · Mantegna · Masaccio · Andrea del Sarto · **Luca della Robbia** (Donatello's designated twin — quattrocento Florentine sculptor, no biographical link to treated units).

**Linked tier (analyzed separately; tests contamination itself):** Verrocchio · Ghirlandaio · Perugino.

**Flagged secondary:** Lorenzo Ghiberti (1.60x growth anomaly; Gates of Paradise restoration/touring 2007–14) · Botticelli (pre-1900 revival complete; diffuse modern exposure) · Fra Angelico (beatified 1982, named patron of artists 1984 — in-window shock) · Giovanni Bellini (cocktail/composer ambiguity).

**Excluded:** Caravaggio (century-long critical rediscovery; own biopic 1986 inside the treatment window).
